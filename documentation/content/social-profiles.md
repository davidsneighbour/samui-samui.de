# Social profile copy

Reusable German profile text for the Samui? Samui! Mastodon and Bluesky accounts. The wording follows the site's author introduction in `src/content/sitewide/authorfooter/index.mdx`: Patrick writes about everyday life on Koh Samui, Thailand, and the things that interest him.

## Short bio

> Samui? Samui! Geschichten und Gedanken von Patrick über das Leben auf Koh Samui und in Thailand. samui-samui.de

## Introduction

> Hallo, ich bin Patrick. Auf Samui? Samui! schreibe ich über meinen Alltag auf Koh Samui, das Leben in Thailand und die Dinge, die mich beschäftigen. Hier teile ich neue Beiträge, Beobachtungen und Geschichten von der Insel. Willkommen! samui-samui.de

## Use

Use the short bio as the profile description. Use the introduction for an introductory or pinned post. If a profile has a separate website field, put `https://samui-samui.de` there and omit the trailing domain from the bio. Keep account handles and platform-specific details outside this shared copy until the accounts are chosen. These texts are drafts saved for reuse; they have not been published.
