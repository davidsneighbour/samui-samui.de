Use the `ss-research-news` skill.

Research Samui, Thailand, and regionally relevant Asia news from the past 10
days: yesterday plus the nine calendar days before yesterday.

Use the maintained source list as an additive starting point, but search independently across Thai-, English-, and German-language sources.

Produce the complete German research brief using the required output structure.

Group the report into the three circles `Samui`, `Thailand`, and
`Asia/Region`, and assign each story an `A`, `B`, or `C` relevance weight.

Pay particular attention to:

* local events;
* infrastructure;
* tourism developments;
* Thailand-wide politics, law, bureaucracy, visas, travel, culture, royalty, and
  public life when useful for a German-language Thailand blog;
* movies, entertainment, celebrities, people coming to Thailand, festivals, and
  events that fit the three circles;
* regional Asia stories that may matter for Thailand, tourism, living as a
  German abroad, public health, safety, or travel flows;
* unusual local stories;
* topics that can be connected to the 21-year history of samui-samui.de.

Do not include general technology topics unless they are materially about life,
travel, public services, infrastructure, scams, censorship, payments, or privacy
in Thailand.

Do not write finished blog posts. Produce research, analysis, and a prioritised shortlist of article ideas.
